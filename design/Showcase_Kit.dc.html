Recipe № 34

Cast-Iron Rosemary Focaccia

Crisp-bottomed, dimpled and rich with olive oil. One 10-inch skillet, four pantry ingredients, and an afternoon of patience.

8
Serves
20m
Prep
2h
Proof
25m
Bake
Ingredients
400 gBread flour
320 gWarm water
8 gFine salt
7 gInstant yeast
3 tbspOlive oil + more for the pan
2 sprigsRosemary
to finishFlaky salt
Method
1
Mix
Whisk flour, salt and yeast. Add the warm water and 2 tbsp oil; stir to a shaggy, sticky dough.
2
First proof
Cover and leave somewhere warm ~2 h, until doubled and bubbled.
3
Oil the skillet
Pour 1 tbsp oil into a 10-inch cast-iron skillet; turn the dough into it.
4
Dimple & rest
Oil your fingers; press deep dimples all over. Rest 20 min while the oven heats.
5
Finish
Drizzle with oil, tuck in rosemary sprigs, and scatter over flaky salt.
6
Bake
220°C / 430°F for 25 min, until deep gold. Cool 10 min before cutting.
Notes — from the kitchen
No cast iron? A cake tin works — just add two minutes. The crumb is even better after an overnight cold proof in the fridge.

Method
1
Mix — whisk flour, salt, yeast; add water + 2 tbsp oil to a shaggy dough.
2
First proof — cover, warm spot, ~2 h until doubled.
3
Oil the skillet — 1 tbsp oil in a 10-inch pan; add dough.
4
Dimple & rest — oiled fingers, deep dimples; rest 20 min.
5
Finish — oil, rosemary, flaky salt.
6
Bake — 220°C / 430°F, 25 min, deep gold. Cool 10 min.
Notes
No cast iron? A cake tin works — add two minutes. Best after an overnight cold proof.
